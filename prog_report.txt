** Overview and Goals ** 
The goal of this project is to develop an intelligent, voice-driven tool to assist in requirements elicitation for software development, 
specifically focusing on classifying requirements into functional, non-functional, and human-centric categories. The tool processes live 
spoken input, transcribes it to text, and applies natural language processing (NLP) techniques for semantic classification and sentiment 
analysis, aiming to capture the tone and emotion behind stakeholder statements to improve understanding.
